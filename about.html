About StoryLooks

StoryLooks shows you what real clothes would look like on you, before you buy them.

What we do

Every week, StoryLooks makes a few photos of you in complete outfits, set in scenes from your own life: your town, your weather, your job, your plans. Every piece in every look is a real item from a real store, in your size and budget, with a link to buy it. You can also ask our style chat what to wear, or have a custom look made for a special moment.

We started StoryLooks because most of us don't shop like fashion ads. We shop for a fall wedding, a game day, a week of work, a Saturday at the farmers market. StoryLooks plans around that, and shows it on you.

See some example looks with real, shoppable outfits.

Who we are

StoryLooks is made by Tysinc LLC, a small company based in Iowa. We're a small team, and a real person reads every email that comes in.

How we make money

  • Shopping links. When you buy something through a link in your looks, StoryLooks may earn a commission from the store, at no extra cost to you.
  • StoryLooks Premium (coming soon): more looks and more custom looks for a monthly price. The free version stays free.

Stores don't pay to be in your weekly looks. Pieces are picked for you: your size, your budget, the weather and what you've told us you like. If we ever add paid placements, they'll be clearly labeled and won't replace your picks.

About the AI

Every StoryLooks image is made by AI from the photos you give us, and each one is labeled as AI-made. An automatic check reviews every image before you see it, for likeness, quality and safety. AI isn't perfect, so a face or a detail is sometimes a little off; your feedback helps it improve. The products are real, but the AI may show a color or fit slightly differently than the store's photos.

Your privacy

Your photos are private. They're never sold, never posted by us, and never used to train AI, and you can delete everything anytime. Read our privacy policy and terms.
