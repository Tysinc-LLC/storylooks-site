How it works

1

People sign up

They share a couple of photos, their sizes, their budget, and a little about their life: work, weekends, what's coming up.

2

We style their week

We build complete outfits from real products in stores' live catalogs, only in stock, in their size, and in their budget, matched to their local weather and plans.

3

They see themselves in it

Each look arrives by email or in our iPhone app. One tap on Shop goes straight to your own product page to purchase.

Why it works for brands

They see it on themselves

Shoppers see your piece on their own body, not a model's. That answers "How will this look on me?", the doubt that stops most people from buying clothes online.

A real reason to buy

Every look is tied to a moment in their week, like a game, a shift or a dinner out, so your product arrives with a story attached.

Traffic straight to you

No marketplace in between. Shoppers land on your site, in your checkout, with your branding.

Every image is checked before anyone sees it

A second AI inspects each finished image, side by side with the shopper's own photos and with your product photo for every piece in the outfit. An image has to pass every check to be sent.

Your product, piece by piece

Each item is compared to your product photo: color, pattern, fabric, cut and details. It also has to be worn the way it's designed, like wide-leg pants falling over boots, not tucked in. A piece that's the wrong color or cut, styled in a way that changes how it looks, or not clearly visible fails the image.

The right person

The shopper has to be clearly recognizable as themselves: face, build, height and age. Close isn't good enough.

A believable, clean photo

Hands, faces and proportions have to look real, and the setting has to make sense for their town and season. No other brands' logos or lettering anywhere in the scene, and always family-friendly.

If an image fails, it's remade. Each look gets up to three tries. If it still doesn't pass, we plan a brand-new look instead. An image that fails the check is never sent, so shoppers only see your products the way they really look.

How we treat your products

  • Always labeled as AI. Every image is clearly marked as made by AI from the shopper's own photos.
  • True to your product. Checked against your product photo every time, as described above.
  • Only people who asked for it. Looks go to people who signed up and confirmed their email. No bought lists, ever.
  • No coupons or discounting. We link to your product page at your price.
  • Your rules. Have brand guidelines, or products you'd rather we leave out? Tell us and we'll follow them.
