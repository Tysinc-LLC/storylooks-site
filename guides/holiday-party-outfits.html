What to wear to a holiday party in the Midwest

December parties around here mean a cold walk from the car, a warm crowded house, and a pile of boots by the door. Here's how to dress for all three and still look like you made an effort.

First, figure out what kind of party it is

  • A house party with friends or family: dressy casual. Dark jeans or nice pants, a sweater or a festive top, and shoes you can slip off.
  • A restaurant or office party: one step up. A dress or a blazer, real shoes, and nothing you'd wear to do chores.
  • A church or community Christmas dinner: somewhere in between. Think what you'd wear on a Sunday, plus something warm.

If the invitation says "ugly sweater," believe it. If it says nothing, aim a little dressier than you think. It's easier to take off a blazer than to wish you had one.

The Midwest rules

  • Dress for the walk, not just the room. Wear a real coat over your outfit, and skip bulky layers underneath. Houses get warm fast once everyone's inside.
  • Plan for shoes off. A lot of Midwest homes ask you to leave shoes at the door, especially when there's snow. Wear socks or tights you don't mind showing, or bring a pair of flats or loafers to change into.
  • Boots that look polished. Leather or suede ankle boots handle a salty driveway and still look right indoors. Save the work boots for the drive.
  • One festive thing is plenty. A deep red or green, a velvet piece, or a little sparkle. One of those looks festive; all of them at once looks like a costume.
  • Dark colors forgive spills. Punch, gravy and kids with frosting are all part of the evening.

Quick answers

Can I wear jeans to a holiday party? At most house parties, yes: dark wash, no rips, with a nicer sweater or top and real shoes. For a restaurant or office party, go with trousers or a dress instead.

What should I do about snow? Wear boots for the walk and bring indoor shoes in a bag. Nobody minds, and your host will appreciate it.

How dressy is "festive attire"? Dressy casual with one holiday touch: a red sweater, a velvet blazer, a sparkly top. You don't need a gown or a suit.
