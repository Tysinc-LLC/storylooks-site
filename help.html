Help

Can't find what you need? Email hello@storylooks.com. A real person reads every message, and we usually reply within a day.

Where your looks come

In the app: a new look drops Monday, Wednesday and Friday at 10 a.m. Central. With App + email, a Saturday email rounds up your week: any looks you might have missed, pieces you hearted, price drops, and a peek at next week. With App only, there are no emails.

By email only: your looks arrive together every Saturday morning. You can choose this on your StoryLooks page (the link at the bottom of any StoryLooks email).

Changes to where you get your looks start with the next week, so the current week stays on its schedule.

Signing in to the app

There's no password. Enter your email, and we'll send a 6-digit code. If it doesn't arrive in a minute or two, check your spam or promotions folder, then tap to send a new one. If you signed up on the website, use the same email in the app; your looks will be there.

Your photos

  • See, add or replace photos: in the app, go to Me → See and add photos. On the website, open your StoryLooks page; to see your current photos there, we'll email you a quick code first (email links can be forwarded, so this keeps your photos private).
  • Best photos: just you, in good daylight, with no sunglasses or hats, plus one full-body photo in clothes that show your build. A few extra photos make your looks more accurate.
  • Your photos are private: never sold, never posted by us, and not viewed by anyone at StoryLooks unless you ask for help or an automatic safety check flags a possible problem. See our Privacy Policy.

When a look isn't right

Every image is made by AI, and AI isn't perfect yet: sometimes a face or a detail comes out a little off. Each look gets an automatic quality check before you see it, and you can always:

  • Rate it. "Does this look like you?" and "Would you wear it?" teach StoryLooks what works for you. After No or Maybe, tell us which pieces didn't work and why.
  • Tap Report a problem on the look. If something is inappropriate, the look is removed right away.
  • Add a few more photos of yourself. It's the biggest single fix for looks that don't quite look like you.

Shopping and favorites

Every piece is a real item from a real store, chosen for your sizes and budget. Tap Shop to see it on the store's own website; any purchase, return or question about an order is with that store. Prices can change. Tap the heart to save a piece to Favorites, and we'll let you know if it goes on sale (you can turn sale alerts off in Me, or on your StoryLooks page). StoryLooks may earn a commission when you buy through our links, at no cost to you.

Sharing a look

Tap Share on any look to send it to friends or post it. Shared images have a small "made with AI" label at the bottom; please keep it on.

Notifications

In the app, go to Me → Notifications on this phone. Turning notifications off stops all StoryLooks notifications, including sale alerts. Your looks will still be waiting in the app on their days.

Change your details

Moved, new sizes, new job, a different budget? In the app, go to Me → Edit my details. On the website, use "Your details" on your StoryLooks page. Your next looks will use the changes. To change your email address, write to hello@storylooks.com.

Take a break, unsubscribe or delete your account

  • Take a break: Me → Take a break in the app, or Pause on your StoryLooks page. Nothing is deleted, and you can come back anytime.
  • Stop emails: use Unsubscribe in any StoryLooks email or on your StoryLooks page. If you use the app, your looks keep coming there.
  • Delete everything: in the app, go to Me → Delete my photos and data. On the website, use "Delete my photos and data" on your StoryLooks page. This permanently deletes your photos, every image we made, and your account, right away. It can't be undone.

Who can use StoryLooks

StoryLooks is for adults 18 and older in the United States, and is free. See our Terms of Service.
