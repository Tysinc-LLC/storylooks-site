STORYLOOKS

Partner agreement

Effective November 25, 2026 · Between you and Tysinc LLC (StoryLooks), 136 S Dubuque Street, Iowa City, IA 52240

This agreement covers the StoryLooks partner program. By accepting approval as a partner, you agree to it, along with our Terms of Service.

1. Joining

You apply, and we approve partners by hand. You must be 18 or older and in the United States. We can decline any application.

2. Your link and code

  • You get a personal link (for example storylooks.com/?ref=sarah) and code (SARAH). Each comes with an offer for your followers that we describe on your dashboard.
  • A member belongs to one partner, decided when they sign up, and that never changes. If they used your link but type a different partner's code, the typed code counts. A link counts for 30 days after the visit, on the same device and browser.
  • Signing yourself up, or people in your own household, doesn't count.

3. What you earn

  • You earn 20% (or the rate in your approval email, if different) of the net revenue StoryLooks actually receives from the members you bring, for 3 years from when each one signs up.
  • Net revenue means StoryLooks Premium payments, one-time purchases such as extra custom picture packs, and shopping commissions from stores whose affiliate programs allow us to share them, after refunds, chargebacks, sales tax and payment-processing fees.
  • Earnings become payable only after the money has cleared and the refund window has passed (about 60 days). Refunded or reversed amounts are taken back.

4. Getting paid

  • We pay quarterly, within 45 days after each quarter ends, once you've earned at least $50 (smaller amounts carry over).
  • Payouts go through Stripe. You'll set up a Stripe account and give the tax information Stripe and the IRS require (for example a W-9). You're responsible for your own taxes; we may send a 1099 where the law requires.

5. Always disclose the partnership

U.S. law (the FTC's endorsement rules) requires you to clearly tell your audience that you earn from StoryLooks, every time you promote it: for example "#ad", "Paid partner of StoryLooks", or "I earn from my StoryLooks link", placed where people will see it (not hidden in hashtags or "see more"). Say honestly what StoryLooks is: an app that makes AI-generated images of you in real outfits. Don't promise results, discounts or features we don't offer.

6. What's not allowed

  • Spam, fake accounts, paying or tricking people to sign up, or anything that inflates your numbers.
  • Bidding on "StoryLooks" in search ads, or pretending to be StoryLooks.
  • Using other people's photos, or images of anyone without their permission.
  • Posting your code on coupon or deal sites without our OK.

If we find any of these, we may withhold earnings connected to them and end your partnership.

7. Privacy

Your dashboard shows counts and money only. You'll never see who your members are, their photos or their looks, and we never share them with you.

8. Our name and images

You may use the StoryLooks name and the materials we give you to promote StoryLooks under this agreement. You may share your own StoryLooks images. Don't change our logo or suggest we endorse something we don't.

9. Ending or changing

Either of us can end this agreement anytime by email. Earnings from members who signed up before it ended, for sign-ups made in line with this agreement, are still paid on the normal schedule once they reach $50. We may change this agreement with at least 30 days' email notice; changes to the earning rate won't apply to members who signed up before the change.

10. The rest

You're an independent partner, not an employee or agent of Tysinc LLC. The program is provided as is. Iowa law applies, and disagreements are handled as described in section 14 of our Terms of Service. Questions: hello@storylooks.com.
