Privacy, in plain English

Last updated November 11, 2026 · See also our Terms of Service.

What we collect

What you give us when you sign up: your name, email, age, zip code, height, clothing sizes, occupation (optional), favorite brands, interests and teams, your photos, and your pet's name and photos if you choose.

What we keep as you use StoryLooks: your ratings, hearts, answers to our quick questions, which pieces you tell us didn't work, and any problems you report; which looks you have opened (so a recap email can lead with ones you missed); which items you tap Shop on (the item, the time, and the kind of device or browser); and, in the iPhone app, a notification address from Apple if you turn notifications on. To stop spam sign-ups we keep a scrambled version of the internet address a sign-up came from, which can't be turned back into the address.

How we use it

  • To make your StoryLooks: AI images of you in real outfits, chosen for your sizes, budget, weather and life.
  • To send your looks by email or in the app, and to tell you when new looks arrive if you turn notifications on.
  • To make future looks better, using your feedback, answers and what you shop.
  • To see which looks and brands people shop, so StoryLooks can work with those brands.

Your photos and the images we make are only sent to you. We never post or share them without asking you first. We don't sell your information, and we don't share it for advertising. StoryLooks doesn't use your photos for face recognition or to identify you.

No one at StoryLooks views your photos or the images we made, except when you ask us for help with them (for example by reporting a problem with a look) or when our automated safety checks flag a possible problem. Then only the person handling it looks, only as needed, and nothing is shared outside StoryLooks.

Your face and body photos

Making images that look like you means using the details of your face and body in your photos. We ask for your written permission for this when you sign up, and we promise:

  • They're used only to make images of you and to check those images look like you.
  • They're never sold, leased or traded, and we never profit from them apart from providing StoryLooks to you.
  • They're not used to identify you or for face recognition.
  • They're shared only with the providers that make and store your looks (Google's AI and Supabase), or if the law requires it.
  • They're deleted right away when you delete your account, automatically after 12 months without using StoryLooks (we email you a month before), and within 7 days if you never confirm your email. Copies in routine backups are erased within 30 days after that.

Who helps us

  • Google (Gemini) makes the images and checks their quality. We use Google's paid service, under which your photos are not used to train or improve Google's products.
  • Supabase stores your information and photos, privately.
  • Resend sends our emails.
  • Apple delivers the iPhone app and its notifications.
  • GitHub hosts this website.

These companies only handle your information to provide their service to StoryLooks.

Shopping links

The clothes in your looks are real products from other stores. When you tap Shop, you go to that store's website, and its own privacy policy applies there. StoryLooks may earn a commission if you make a purchase, at no cost to you. Brands shown aren't affiliated with or endorsing StoryLooks unless we say so.

How long we keep it

We keep your information while you have a StoryLooks account. If you pause or unsubscribe, your photos stay saved in case you come back, until you delete them or 12 months pass without you using StoryLooks. When you delete (or we do after 12 months), your photos, every image we made, your answers, hearts and shopping taps, and your account are deleted right away. Sign-ups that are never confirmed are deleted within 7 days.

Your choices and rights

Every StoryLooks email links to your own page, and the app has a Me tab, where you can add photos, pause, unsubscribe, turn notifications off, or delete everything. Deleting is permanent and immediate.

You can also ask us for a copy of the information we have about you, or ask us to correct it. Email hello@storylooks.com and we'll take care of it. Some states, like California, give their residents these rights by law; we offer them to everyone.

Do Not Track

StoryLooks doesn't track you across other websites or use advertising trackers, so there's nothing different to switch off when your browser sends a "Do Not Track" signal.

Keeping it safe

Your information and photos are stored privately and sent over encrypted connections. Only the systems that make your looks, and the StoryLooks team when needed, can reach them.

Age

StoryLooks is only for adults 18 and older.

Changes

If we change how we use your information, we'll update this page and tell you by email before it applies to you.

Questions
