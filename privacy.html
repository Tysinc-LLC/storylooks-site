Privacy, in plain English

Last updated November 9, 2026

What we collect

What you give us when you sign up: your first name, email, age, zip code, height, clothing sizes, occupation (optional), favorite brands, interests and teams, your photos, and your pet's name and photos if you choose. We also keep the feedback and answers you tap in your emails.

How we use it

  • To make your StoryLooks: AI images of you in real outfits, chosen for your sizes, budget, weather and life.
  • To send you your StoryLooks emails.
  • To make future looks better, using your feedback and answers.

Your photos and the images we make are only sent to you. We never post or share them without asking you first. We don't sell your information.

While StoryLooks is being tested, the small StoryLooks team may look at the images we make, and the photos used to make them, to check quality and fix problems. They stay private and are never shared outside StoryLooks.

Who helps us

  • Google (Gemini) makes the images and checks their quality. We use Google's paid service, under which your photos are not used to train or improve Google's products.
  • Supabase stores your information and photos, privately.
  • Resend sends our emails.

Shopping links

The clothes in your looks are real products from other stores. When you tap Shop, you go to that store's website. StoryLooks may earn a commission if you make a purchase, at no cost to you.

Your choices

Every StoryLooks email has a link to your own page, where you can add photos, pause, unsubscribe, or delete everything: your photos, every image we made, and your account. Deleting is permanent and immediate.

Age

StoryLooks is only for adults 18 and older.

Questions

Email us any time at hello@storylooks.com.
